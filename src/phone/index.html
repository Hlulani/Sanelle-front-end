How Sanelle AI stays safe

Consent, checks and privacy
  • Nothing is sent until she agrees, and she's told exactly what's read.
  • Drafts that diagnose, name a cause, invent a number or misquote her are removed.
  • Nothing is saved until she taps Add or Save. Health records stay on her device.
  • Not medical advice: Sanelle helps her prepare; her clinician decides.

The server can take up to a minute to wake on the first sign-in.
