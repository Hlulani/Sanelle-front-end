A two-minute path

  1. Tap I already have an account and log in.
  2. Today: choose Understand my scan.
  3. My health: add a report, then see what it leaves out.
  4. Food: plan a week and swap a meal.
  5. Appointment: your questions and summary.

Limits, honestly

Not medical advice. Report reading copies text for you to check; it doesn’t interpret it. Health records stay in this browser.
