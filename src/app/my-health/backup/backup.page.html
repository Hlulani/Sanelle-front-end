Keep a backup

Bring your health records with you if you change phones or browsers.

Your records live on this device

Signing in on another device does not bring them across. Clearing app or browser storage can remove them.

A backup includes report findings and history, questions and answers, check-ins, visit notes, next steps and summary choices. Original report files and meal plans are not included.

Save an encrypted backup

Choose a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters. You will need it to restore the file; Sanelle cannot recover it.

Restore a backup

First unlock the file and review what is inside. Nothing is replaced until you confirm.
