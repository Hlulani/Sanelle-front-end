# Meal photo credits

All **166 current recipes** have an explicit photo match, using **93 bundled photos** (including one neutral fallback for future recipes). The library retains the previous 33 photos and adds 60 sourced online on 4 October 2026.

Photos are serving suggestions rather than a record of the exact cooked recipe. Similar variants can share a photo. Some hard-to-match recipes use a labelled ingredient photo; the match table records those differences. Recipe ingredients define the meal.

Today, day/week plans, swaps, quick meals, related meals and recipe details share image handling. Backend images are honoured, broken links fall back to the matched local photo, and future unmapped recipes use labelled meal inspiration. Local photos load without reaching stock-photo sites.

Pexels and ordinary Unsplash photos use their respective licences; no Unsplash+ photos are included. Commons photos retain their individual CC/public-domain terms. Recipe pages show source/photographer and licence links for the image actually loaded. Images are resized for display and may be cropped by the screen layout; CC BY-SA images retain their licences. Credits from the original set were retained.
